Understanding Support and Resistance

Support and resistance are foundational concepts in technical analysis that represent price levels where market forces tend to prevent further movement in a particular direction.

Resistance: Where selling pressure increases
Support: Where buying pressure increases

Support is a price level where buying interest is sufficiently strong to overcome selling pressure. As price declines and approaches support, buyers enter the market, creating a "floor" that prevents the price from falling further.

Resistance is a price level where selling interest overcomes buying pressure. As price rises and approaches resistance, sellers enter the market, creating a "ceiling" that prevents the price from rising further.

Key Characteristics

  • Role Reversal: Once broken, support can become resistance and vice versa
  • Strength Correlation: The more times a level is tested without breaking, the stronger it becomes
  • Volume Confirmation: High volume at support/resistance levels adds validity
  • Zones vs. Exact Levels: Consider support and resistance as zones rather than precise price points

Trading Applications

Bounce Trading

Enter long positions when price bounces off support or enter short positions when price rejects from resistance. Use a tight stop loss just beyond the level that was respected.

Breakout Trading

Enter positions when price convincingly breaks through support or resistance. Look for increased volume and momentum to confirm the validity of the breakout.

Range Trading

When price is consolidating between clear support and resistance levels, trade the range by buying at support and selling at resistance until a breakout occurs.
